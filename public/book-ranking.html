The Best Books According to Leonna

My top 3 books of all-time.

The Hobbit by J.R.R. Tolkein

Growing up with an immigrant mother meant my father had to teach us all how to speak English. Since he worked overnights at the powerplant, the only real time he could spend with my siblibings and I was right before he would leave for work. The Hobbit was the first story I remember him telling me before I went to bed. I was fascinated by Bilbo and his dwarven friends as they journeyed to fight a dragon. This book was my introduction to the fantasy genre and I have been chasing dragons ever since

The Kingdom of the Wicked series by Kerri Maniscalco

This series was my my favorite Persephone/Hades retelling, I love the inclusion of Italian heritage and the mention of the Cornicello. My mother was born in Italy, so I grew up with stories about her village. She would also wear her cornicello daily so it was a constant presence in my life.

The Plated Prisioner Series by Raven Kennedy

The Retelling of King Midas with a twist, Auren pulls you in with her mysterious past as well as her caged presence. You can't help but want to know why she is in a cage with a King who clearly only uses her for some nefarious purpose or another. It's a story about growth and self discovery. About a woman who sheds the things that no longer serve her and becomes the goddess she never knew she could be.
